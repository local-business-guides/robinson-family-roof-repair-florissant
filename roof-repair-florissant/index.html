What a repair discussion can cover

The repair process depends on what a professional finds. Start with the symptoms you’ve observed rather than a guess about the cause. The contractor can assess the roof and discuss whether a repair can address the concern or if replacement or other work may be more suitable.

Before the visit, prepare a few details:
- Identify the room or exterior area where you noticed a change.
- Note when it started and whether it has appeared more than once.
- Take photos from a safe location if you already have them.
- Mention any previous roofing work you know about.
- Make a list of questions about the proposed work and what comes next.

Don’t patch or alter an area on the roof if you aren’t equipped to do that work safely. A quick attempt can make it harder to understand the original concern. Avoid assuming that a visible symptom points to one specific repair. Let the assessment guide the discussion, and ask for an explanation before agreeing to a scope of work.
