What to ask and prepare before work

The right scope depends on the roof and the work it needs. A conversation may lead to a repair, replacement, installation, or maintenance plan. Ask what the work is intended to address and what you should expect during the process. If you’re considering a larger project, ask how the recommendation relates to the condition you’ve observed.

A short checklist can make your first call more useful:
- Note where you see a concern, inside or outside the property.
- Write down when you first noticed it and whether anything has changed.
- Gather any photos you already have, but don’t climb onto the roof to take more.
- Mention earlier roofing work if you know what was done.
- Ask what happens next and what areas of the property may need access.

Avoid naming a cause before the roof has been assessed. A ceiling mark or loose-looking material can be a clue, but it doesn’t tell the full story by itself. Also avoid trying an unfamiliar roof repair on your own. A professional can evaluate the issue and discuss suitable work.
